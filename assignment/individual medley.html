Individual Medley:


Individual Medley, or IM is the combination of all 4 strokes. Butterfly is swum first, followed by backstroke, breaststroke and freestyle respectively.
IM is swum at 3 different lengths:

  • - 100 Metres
  • - 200 Metres
  • - 400 Metres

    • A good IM swimmer is Leon Marchand.
